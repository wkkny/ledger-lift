# Ledger Lift

Ledger Lift turns a photo of a handwritten sales ledger into rows a shop owner can review and export.

## Language

**Owner**:
The shop owner who keeps the ledger and uses Ledger Lift on their own computer.
_Avoid_: User, customer

**Ledger page**:
A photographed page from a shop's handwritten daily sales book.
_Avoid_: Document, scan

**Entry**:
One sale recorded on a ledger page, with a date, item or description, quantity, and amount.
_Avoid_: Record, transaction

**Review**:
A person's check and correction of extracted entries before export.
_Avoid_: Approval, verification

**CSV**:
A downloadable comma-separated file containing the entries from a ledger page.
_Avoid_: Spreadsheet (unless referring to the app used to open it)
